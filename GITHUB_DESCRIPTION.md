## Short Description (350 char max - appears under the repo name)
A DIY Bluetooth item tracker built entirely from salvaged electronics. No microcontroller, no firmware, no new parts - just a discarded earbud, a scrap speaker, and a companion Android app that alerts you when you leave something behind or helps you find it with a 3000 Hz locator beacon.
